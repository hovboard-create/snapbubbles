— THE STORY

Why we
rebuilt a
dead 2005
domain.

Snapbubbles.com ran a Flash-based bubble wrap game in the early 2000s. Adobe killed Flash in December 2020 and the site went dark. We rebuilt it in HTML5 — same core idea, same satisfying pop, but now with three modes, proper mobile support, and procedurally synthesized audio.

The bubble wrap design dates back to 1957, when two engineers in New Jersey were trying to invent textured wallpaper. It flopped. They pivoted to greenhouse insulation. That flopped too. Then IBM needed packaging for the 1401 computer. Suddenly bubble wrap had a market — and decades later, an accidental cultural object.

This site is one tiny extension of that lineage.
